[launch.usage]
Usage: {1}/liltress <command>

  configure   prepares global.conf and secrets.conf
  check       checks those two files
  run         installs (root)
  lynis       Lynis audit (root)
  logs        copies the journals
  vpn         WireGuard or OpenVPN tunnel (root)
  verify      checks the installed product (root)
  maintain    rebuilds a baseline or repeats upkeep (root)
  timer       runs a scheduled workstation check (root)
  cron        runs a scheduled server check (root)
  install     prepares packages or the clone
  help        this help
